#Modified IL grading function:
data_rep_coeff * rank_coeff * manual_grade * outcome_mult



Outcome_mult: //treat 3 and 4 stocks as the same, since garbage replays will overrepresent 4-stocks and either is a comically large margin in a competitively-matched game
  0.7 + 0.2 if stocks remaining >= 1 + 0.1 * (max of stocks_remaining or 3) 

Rank_coeff:
  0.3 + (sum_peak_elo - 3000)/2000 + (charOI_eloxe_2800 * 2)
  Where charOI_eloxe_2800 = 1 / (1 + 10*( (2800 - peak_elo) / 400))

  Replays without rank data assume ELO 1800 (lowest included in dataset)

data_rep_coeff:
  1 - (player_replays/total_replays)^2

manual_grade = {
  LESSON: 4
  ROBUST_MODERN: 1.4
  OLD_SCHOOL: 1.2
  STRONG_MORON: 1
  MID_LVL: 0.8
  SCRAP: 0.6
}
  
e.g.,
MEKK: representing ~80% of available labelled replays has coeff
0.36 [data rep] * 1 [STRONG_MORON label] * 1.8 [rank coeff assuming an average match is 2500 vs 2300] * 1 [outcome coeff assuming average stock diff ~+1] = ~0.65
And thus will likely make up ~50% of total training weight

In reward.py, consider slightly increasing approaching, anti-stall, and anti-ledgegrab factors from their default zero to match their values in the ratings-embed fork (used in current state of the art Phillip agents), as these are likely high value but undesirable strategies and will be significantly represented in the initial imitation dataset.
